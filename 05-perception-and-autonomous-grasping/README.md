# 05 · Perception and Autonomous Grasping

## Scope

Follow the information needed to locate an object, guide an approach, establish contact, and maintain a grasp. Then examine how demonstrations can describe motions relative to objects and people.

## Learning objectives

- Explain stereo depth, RGB-D data, surface segmentation, and grasp candidates.
- Distinguish position-based, image-based, and hybrid visual servoing.
- Interpret contact-force and slip measurements, including filtering tradeoffs.
- Explain why grasp force may need online adjustment.
- Describe task frames, time alignment, and Gaussian mixture models in learning from demonstration.

## Prerequisites

[Coordinate frames and kinematics](../03-kinematics-foundations/README.md) and [control and interaction](../04-control-and-interaction/README.md). Equations use simple geometry and scalar time signals; no vision library or robot hardware is required.
