# 04 · Control and Interaction

## Scope

Connect motion goals to feedback, then examine how robots regulate contact and share workspaces with people. The scalar equations are teaching models with explicit assumptions, not complete hardware controllers.

## Learning objectives

- Distinguish open-loop commands, servo feedback, and controller layers.
- Separate setpoint regulation, path following, and trajectory tracking.
- Explain force, impedance, and hybrid motion–force control.
- Distinguish collaborative applications from robot geometry and describe the limits of passivity claims.

## Prerequisites

[Robot systems](../01-robotics-foundations/README.md), [motion and DOF](../02-manipulators-and-motion/README.md), and [kinematics](../03-kinematics-foundations/README.md). A dot over a variable denotes its rate of change; two dots denote acceleration.
